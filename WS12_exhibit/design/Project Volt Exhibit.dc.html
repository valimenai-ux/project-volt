PREMISE

A program that killed its own favourite ideas, on criteria written before the numbers existed.

Two vehicles went on trial. A 6,600 kg delivery truck became a pure series hybrid — the diesel never touches the wheels. A 36,300 kg semi tried four hybrid architectures and every one lost to a modern diesel once the freight it could no longer carry was counted.

Nothing here was adopted by preference. Each gate carried a numeric kill criterion, armed before the computation ran. Two of those criteria killed the program's own preferred designs. Both results are on this wall.
