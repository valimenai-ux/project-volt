# SUPERSEDED — pre-record design draft, kept as visual provenance only

`Project Volt Exhibit.dc.html` and `support.js` are the **original
dc-runtime design draft**, written before the exhibit was bound to the
record. They are **not** part of the exhibit and are **not** served: they
sit outside `app/`, Vite never sees them, and nothing here is deployed.

**They contain material the exhibit exists to have removed**, and it is
left in place unedited so the draft remains an honest record of what was
superseded:

- a `RATIFIED RECORD` badge — a promoted status, forbidden in the exhibit
  under `BASELINE_v7_FREEZE.md` R52;
- a `BASELINE v4 · RATIFIED 2026-08-30` header — the exhibit is bound to
  **v7 and frozen**;
- a seeded synthetic trace generator, engine model and duty-cycle builder,
  and hard-coded r2-vintage candidate constants that are wrong against the
  r3 record.

**Superseded by `../app/`.** Nothing in this folder may be quoted as a
result, a status, or a number of record. What survives from it is the
visual system — palette, typography, panel grid, badge tiers, provenance
strip — ported into `../app/src/theme.ts` and documented in
`../REPORT_WS12.md` §1.

Recorded at the citation-check adjudication's request (`FINDINGS_WS12_r1.md`,
WS12-r1-m7).
