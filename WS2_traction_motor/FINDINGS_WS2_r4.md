## 3. New findings

### WS2-F13 — minor. `interface.electrical_ratings.inverter_Tj_at_peak_C` is a single-case scalar evaluated at nominal voltage; its own sibling field's rule says the window ceiling governs, and there the peak-tier worst case is 135 °C, not the exported 131 °C

**What is wrong.** The continuous-tier Tj is properly R14-enumerated over the window voltages ("switching loss rises with bus voltage"), governing case 749 V, value 133 °C. One line below, the peak-tier Tj is exported as the bare scalar 131 °C — which is the 662.4 V member only (my re-derivation: 1,138 W → 131.4 °C at nominal; 1,200 W → **135.0 °C** at the 748.8 V ceiling). The export therefore understates the worst-case peak junction temperature by ~4 K and, read naively, implies peak running is cooler than continuous running when at matched voltage it is hotter. This is the exact defect class R14 exists to kill — a worst-case-shaped field whose enumeration is missing the governing member — surviving in the round that implemented R14.

**Why it is only minor.** Nothing consumes this field for sizing; 135 °C is comfortably inside any automotive 1200 V SiC Tj rating (150–175 °C); no conclusion, rating tier, or cable moves. Report §2.3's prose ("Tj 133 °C at the continuous point at the window ceiling, 131 °C at peak") matches the export, so the three-way agreement holds — the gap is in the case set, not in transcription.

**Resolution.** Enumerate `inverter_Tj_at_peak_C` over the window voltages exactly like the continuous tier (governing: 749 V, 135 °C), or at minimum label the field with its evaluation voltage.

### WS2-F14 — minor. The genset→bus run's enumerated continuous set tops out at "R6 125 kW sustained" (289.4 A), but the ratified R18 genset can sustain ~128 kW bus-side (~296 A at the floor) — a member above the labeled governing case; same cable either way

**What is wrong.** R18 ratifies a 132 kW continuous flat-rated genset; through the genset-side 0.97 conditioning (R12) that is ~128 kW bus-side sustainable at sea level — 296.3 A at the 432.0 V floor, above the set's 289.4 A governing member. Under R14 the enumerated set should contain the source's actual maximum sustained output (or state why the 125 kW requirement bounds delivery). Consequence-free in hardware: 296 A still clears the chosen 120 mm² / 320 A run with 8.0% margin, so the gauge, mass, and totals are unchanged. In the same spirit but even smaller: the chopper run's transient list omits the 800 V hardwired-backstop event (214.3 A, brief) — it clears the 225 A ampacity trivially.

**Resolution.** Add an "R18 genset max continuous output, bus-side, at the floor" member (~296 A) to the genset-run case set — it becomes the governing case with the same 120 mm² answer — or annotate why delivery is capped at 125 kW; optionally add the 800 V backstop row to the chopper run's transient cases.
