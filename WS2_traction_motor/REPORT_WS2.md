## 11. First-principles sanity checks

1. **Torque chain (unchanged).** 13,500 N × 0.37 m = 4,995 Nm wheel;
   / 10 / 0.97 = 515 Nm shaft; capability 530 Nm = 2.9% margin.
   100 km/h ⇒ 7,169 rpm, inside 7,200 by 31 rpm.
2. **String arithmetic IS the window.** 288 × 1.5 / 2.3 / 2.6 / 2.7 V
   = 432.0 / 662.4 / 748.8 / 777.6 V — verified in code
   (`bus.string_check`), and 12 cells × 2.3 V = 27.6 V granularity.
3. **Rewind invariance, demonstrated not asserted.** At matched
   per-unit points the loss members carry to the hundredth: crawl V1
   copper 6.76 / iron 0.90 kW and crawl V2 copper 6.91 / iron 2.20 kW
   — identical to r3's members at 370 V — and spin drag at 85 km/h is
   1,109 W in both windows. Only the inverter member moved (1.05 →
   1.10 kW; different device class), which is exactly the one
   component that was *not* rewound.
4. **The R13 corner closes by hand.** MTPA for 515 Nm needs 648.1 A_pk
   on the r3 machine ⇒ 648.1/1.47 = 440.9 A_pk here; at 1,792 rpm
   (25 km/h) the flux ~0.209 Wb × 1.47 = 0.307 Wb needs ω_e·ψ ≈
   751 × 0.307 ≈ 231 V + resistive ≈ 236.9 V — exactly the 0.95 ×
   432/√3 = 236.9 V controller limit. The corner is voltage-exact by
   construction of the ×1.47 factor.
5. **Peak power vs voltage is monotone and sane.** 121.2 → 188.0 →
   212.9 kW across 432/662/749 V; DC side closes: peak draw at the
   floor 130.7 kW = 302 A at 432 V, under the 320 A genset-run
   ampacity class and far under the pack+genset supply.
6. **Copper loss closes by hand.** S2 at 662.4 V: 233 A_pk ⇒ 1.5 ×
   22.2 mΩ (150 °C) × 233² × 1.184 (AC at 291.5 Hz) ≈ 2.14 kW — the
   model books 2.14 kW. The iron member 2.64 kW at the S2 point
   exceeds r3's because the machine holds ~full flux at nominal (no
   deep FW at 61 km/h in the new window) — the physical cause of the
   ledger-seed rises in §8.
7. **Resistor energy balance.** 150.22 kW = 748.8²/3.73248 and
   162.0 kW = 777.6²/3.73248 = 3.24 × 50 kW exactly; air side
   150.2 kW / (1.005 kJ/kgK × 150 K) = 0.996 kg/s = 3,261 m³/h ✓;
   ribbon 361 °C at design from h·A = 60 × 10.37 ✓; altitude
   compounding 361 → 431 °C at σ = 0.79 ✓; 10-s transient ΔT =
   (162.0−150.2) kW × 10 s / (16.4 kg × 0.5 kJ/kgK) = 14.4 K ✓;
   cable ceiling 225² × 3.73248 = 189.0 kW ✓.
8. **The crawl loss set closes member-wise.** Band top − V1 = 10.57 −
   8.79 = 1.78 kW = iron +1.47 (0.90 → 2.37 kW, 50.7 → 119.5 Hz at
   the same crawl flux factor) + copper +0.26 (648.1 vs 643.9 A_pk
   MTPA and AC growth) + windage +0.04 + inverter +0.01 ✓.
9. **UCG moves the right way.** EMF(7,200 rpm) = 407.4 V × 1.47 =
   599 V: above a 599 V bus there is no UCG below the speed spec —
